Date: Thursday, April 30th

Time: 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM

## Theme: 
Free-style arrangement emphasizing the color of the vase conducted by Jeff Fine.
Check Text book 3-10(page 30-32)
Bring your own vase, materials, and if you need a kenzan, please bring it.

Note:

Check the container's colors carefully.
Think and select what combination of materials: Which compositional approach can make the most of the container's colors?
Could be the colors in the same tonal or contrast. Check book 3-8 and 3-9 (pages 24-29).


Bring food to share with others.

There will be a quick general meeting about them at the next workshop on June 18th. Also, the Sogetsu 100th anniversary event.
