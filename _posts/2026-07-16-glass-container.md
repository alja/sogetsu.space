## Theme
Free style arrangement using a transparent or translucent glass container. Your materials and water need to be show through the glass. The work can be composed inside or outside the container. You can check book 4-12 page 86 & 87. 5-13 page 66-69. Bring own materials, container, no kenzan.

Please bring a dish of foods to share with friends.
