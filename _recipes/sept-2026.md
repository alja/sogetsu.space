## Date: Monday, Sep.14th
Place: Casa Del Prado Room 104
Time: 10:00 - 3:00, Please be there 15 minutes before WS starts.
Fees: $80 plus $5 cash (for materials, utensils, paper products and rent.)

## Theme:
AM: Dissembling and Rearranging the Materials using Willow or grass
materials if possible. Check text book 4-9, Page 78&79
( Arrangement in which the original appearance of the materials changed)
Bring own materials either grass or willow if possible, flowers for color. Bring
vase, Kenzan if you need to use.

PM: Abstract composition using the cardboard core of empty roll of toilet paper, paper
towel or wrapping paper. Bring 10 or more of them. Bring leaves, flowers.
Glue, scotch tapes, staples, wire, plastic water bottle or orchid holder for holding
water.

Bring a dish of foods to share with others.
