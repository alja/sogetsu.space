#### Sept 2024
AM: Free Style Arrangement using Corn Husks

Materials: Corn Husk, 5-10 stems of branches or twigs either fresh or dry, if your stems are short, bring 15-20. Bring flowers and greenery. 

Vase: Glass container.

Tools: Wire, stapler, tape, etc, whatever you need.

#### May 2024 
AM: Using one kind of material:
Green leaves only, you can use Iris green leaves as well (not yellow or white ones). Check Text book 4-4 P 68&69, 5-4 P22-25 
Bring own materials, vase and tools. 
 
PM: 
Disassembling and Reassembling the materials: Using one kind of branch with leaves. After you finish it, can be add the flowers for color. Check Text book 4-9, P 78&79. Bring own materials, vase and tools.
