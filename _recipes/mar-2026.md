#### AM:
Free-style arrangement using large leaves and wrapping papers. Please bring your own leaves, such as Monstera, Japanese Aralia, Bird of Paradise, Calla Lily, etc. Bring papers, scissors, a stapler, scotch tape, and glue if you need. Bring a free-style vase and a kenzan, if you need to use them.
### PM:
Exhibition arrangements. Bring your own sketches, materials, a vase, and Kenzan if needed. Whether you participate in the Exhibition or not, you can still do this subject. Check Book 5-25.
Please bring a dish of food to share with others.
Please mark your calendar for this year’s Workshop.
