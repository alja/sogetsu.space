## Theme

### AM: Thanksgiving centerpiece using multiple containers

See textbook 5-3, pages 19 to 21.

Bring your own flowers, dried leaves and greenery, several small vases (not

### PM: Free style arrangement using paper egg cartons

See textbook 4-11, pages 82 to 85, and 5-7, pages 36 to 38.

Patricia will provide each of us with one purple egg carton. If you would like
to use more, please bring your own paper egg cartons.

a stapler, glue, Scotch tape, and wire if you need it. Bring a contemporary
vase and your Ikebana essentials.

Please bring a dish of food to share with everyone.
