Time: November 10th, 10:00 - 3:00 ( Please be there 15 minutes before WS starts)

Place: Casa Del Prado RM 104

Fees: $80 ( $45 if you attend only one session) plus $5 cash for (rent, tea, coffee, and disposable plates and cups)




Themes:

### AM: Free-style arrangement with making one's own shallow basket.

Bring: Branches (Twigs), Flax, or Grass materials that are easy to weave. Flowers,

branches, or greens. Bring your own vase, place it into a basket, Kenzan, if you need to

use.



{% include gallery %}


### PM: Practicing for future demonstrations

Build arrangement from the back. You can select either Moribana, Nageire, or Freestyle.

for a future demonstration.

Bring the Materials, the Vase, and Kenzan accordingly.
