## Date:
Monday, May 12th
Time: 10:00 am to 3:00 pm, Please arrive there at least 15 minutes before workshop starts, so you can pay the fees and get ready for session.
Place: Casa Del Prado, room 104
Fees: $80 for both AM and PM workshop, if you attend only one session will be $45

## Theme:
### AM: Kabuwake arrangement using a large Moribana (shallow) vase
Please check a text Book 3-3 Page 10-13, Bring own branches, flowers and
leaves. Bring 2 Kenzan and a large shallow moribana vase.
If you don’t have a large shallow vase, you can use a large tray, casserole dish,
etc. Please be creative!

### PM: Emphasizing water using same a shallow vase from AM session
Bring own materials, vine like Ivy, leaves and flowers.

Bring foods to share with others.
